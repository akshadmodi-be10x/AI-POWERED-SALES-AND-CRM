Apply the 5 fixes (tap each)

✓
Fix 1 — A subject that creates curiosity

"Business Proposal" = instant delete. Be specific about THEIR world.

✓
Fix 2 — A real person, not Sir/Madam

Write to the events manager by role. Nobody is "Dear Sir/Madam".

✓
Fix 3 — One researched detail

Mention something true about them — proof you did homework.

✓
Fix 4 — Proof in numbers, not adjectives

"world-class passionate" says nothing. "40 weddings, 2-hr setup" says everything.

✓
Fix 5 — One small, specific ask

"Revert at earliest" asks for nothing. Ask for 15 minutes, with a day.
