The 70% Problem — As a Comic 🎨

Sales Series · AI-Powered Sales & CRM Week

Today's 5-minute mission

Meet TiffinBox Daily — a small tiffin-service startup where one sales rep handles every lead. Today you turn their (very familiar) struggle into a comic strip — using AI.

  • 1Open Gemini (gemini.google.com) — free account is fine.
  • 2Copy the comic prompt on the right and paste it in. Generate.
  • 3Not happy with one panel? Just tell Gemini what to change — "make panel 4 funnier".
  • 4Share your comic in the group. Funniest panel wins bragging rights till Friday. 🏆
💡 Why a comic? Because by Friday's live session, you'll know exactly how to DELETE panels 2, 3 and 4 from your own working day.
COPY THIS PROMPT INTO GEMINI 
Create a 6-panel comic strip, flat modern vector style, light blue and white colour palette with orange accents, clean rounded characters, big readable speech bubbles, titled "THE 70% PROBLEM".

Story — one sales rep at a small tiffin-service startup called TiffinBox Daily:

Panel 1: Monday 9 AM. The rep at a tidy desk, coffee in hand, thought bubble: "Today I will close 3 deals!"
Panel 2: 11 AM. Same rep drowning behind a laptop with 15 browser tabs, papers flying, speech bubble: "Still researching the FIRST lead…"
Panel 3: 2 PM. The rep typing an email that starts "Dear Sir/Madam…" — on the client's screen, the email flying straight into a trash bin.
Panel 4: 5 PM. A wall of sticky notes behind the rep, one note falling off that says "FOLLOW UP!!". Rep looks horrified: "I forgot the hot lead!"
Panel 5: A friendly little AI robot assistant appears, hands the rep ONE neat page labelled "Prospect Brief", while the rep's phone buzzes: "Client replied!"
Panel 6: The rep shaking hands with a happy customer over a tiffin box, relaxed and smiling. Caption at the bottom: "Give the 70% to AI. Keep the 30% that pays."

No real brand names or logos, no real people, no flags. Keep it lighthearted and office-humour funny.
